How to use Slice Horn Lab

Draw the cabinet's side view as 18 mm / ¾" boards, set the width, drop in a driver, and it simulates it with a Hornresp-style 1-D model: SPL, impedance, excursion, group delay, step response, waterfall and max SPL.

Quick start

  1. It opens with an 18" tapped horn so you can see how it works. Start from (top) has more presets.
  2. Pick a driver at the top: filter by Brand and Size, or search by model. Import driver… takes a Hornresp file, WinISD .wdr or a spec sheet PDF.
  3. Edit the cabinet here on the left. The curves update as you drag.
  4. Read the results on the tabs above the graph (SPL, Impedance, Excursion, Group delay, Step, Decay, Max SPL…).
  5. File → Download design saves your work. It also auto-saves in this browser.

Drawing

  • P draw a board (click-drag). Snaps to the grid, corners and faces. Grid units at the top: 1", 0.1", cm, mm.
  • V select. Drag a board's end to stretch it (joined ends follow; Shift = that end only), its middle to move it, anywhere else to slide it sideways.
  • D driver: click a board to mount it. F flips it. Right-click it for front/rear mounting.
  • Right-click a corner to change how two boards join.
  • Design panel (right side): internal width, board thickness, floor / wall / corner loading.
  • Air paths are found automatically. You don't need to draw them.
  • Ctrl Z undo · wheel zooms · right-drag pans · 0 fit · ? this help.

Handy extras

  • Power slider (left of the graph, 1 W–10 kW, on every tab): scales SPL, excursion and air velocity; anything past Xmax turns red.
  • DSP bar: HPF/LPF (Butterworth, LR, Bessel up to 48 dB/oct) + 6 PEQs, with the filter curve shown in green.
  • Target: drag orange EQ-style handles to the response you want, then Auto-tune re-design reshapes the cabinet to match.
  • Pin curve to compare before and after.
  • Quantize the build (Design panel) rounds everything to whole mm and 5° angles before you cut.
  • Export: cut list, DXF side profile, CSV response, and the equivalent Hornresp inputs to cross-check.
  • Presets: 18" tapped horn (Speaker Box Maker), 1850 folded horn (Rog Mogale), SKRAM 21" and Skhorn 2×21" (Josh Ricci), BC118 (All_1), Super Scooper 18" (Rog Mogale), plus tapped horn / FLH / vented / sealed templates.

Limits

It's a 1-D plane-wave model like Hornresp (checked against Hornresp exports: 0.00 dB on a front-loaded horn, ~0.05 dB on a vented box). It doesn't model panel flex, port compression or driver non-linearity, so treat the red over-excursion areas as "not actually reachable". The preset designs are traced approximations for simulation; build from the designers' own plans.
